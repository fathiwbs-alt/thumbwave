FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do you need from me to start? +
Your channel name, the title or topic of your next video, and any style notes — colors, mood, references, things you love or want to avoid. One chat message is enough; your pack is built from it.
How is a pack delivered? +
Straight to your Whop chat as a ZIP: the three full-res PNG concepts plus a preview sheet so you can compare the whole set at a glance. Upload the files as-is — every size is already correct.
What size are the thumbnails? +
Every concept ships at 1280 × 720 — YouTube's exact thumbnail size, at full resolution. No resizing, no reformatting, no edits needed.
Are revisions included? +
Yes. If a concept misses the mark, send your notes within 7 days of delivery and it gets reworked — included with every pack, at no extra charge.
When does my pack land? +
Your pack is designed from the brief you send — typically delivered to your chat within ~3 days. Want thumbnails for another video later? Just buy another pack.
How does buying again work? +
Every purchase is a single one-time pack. When you want thumbnails for another video, buy another pack — nothing renews automatically, and nothing else is charged. Every file you receive is yours to keep.
